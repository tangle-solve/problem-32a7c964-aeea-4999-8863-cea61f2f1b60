### CHEM-000185: The simultaneous DOE ultimate PEM electrolyser target
- **Subfield:** Water splitting, hydrogen production and fuel cells
- **Open:** Meet the official ultimate targets on one practical stack: 3 A cm⁻² at 1.6 V per cell, 0.125 mg cm⁻² total platinum-group metals and 80,000-hour lifetime, alongside cost requirements.
- **Status:** This is a combined target. A 2025 study achieved below 1.75 V at 4 A cm⁻² with 0.375 mg Ir cm⁻², advancing only some constraints.
- **Type:** moonshot
- **Why:** It would reduce renewable hydrogen’s material and capital intensity.
